# Water Hardness Lab (EDTA Titration)

An interactive virtual chemistry lab that simulates the determination of the hardness of water by EDTA complexometric titration.

Made for **Chemistry for Computer Science (26CYB1002J)**, Unit 2 practice, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Tiruchirappalli.

**Live demo:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/water-hardness-lab/

## What you can do

1. **Learn:** read the aim, principle and endpoint colour change.
2. **Prepare:** calculate the mass of standard hard water (CaCO3) and EDTA needed. The concentrations are editable.
3. **Standardize EDTA:** drag the burette, pipette and flask into place, add buffer and EBT indicator, then titrate until the flask turns from wine red to blue.
4. **Test your water:** repeat the titration with a water sample.
5. **Report:** see your readings and the result, then print or save the report as a PDF.

Each time the page loads, the sample gets a different hidden hardness, so every student gets different readings.

## Calculation

Hardness (ppm CaCO3) = (V2 / V1) x 1000

- V1 = mean EDTA titre for 20 mL of standard hard water
- V2 = mean EDTA titre for 20 mL of the water sample

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. There is nothing to install.

## Note

This is an educational simulation. Readings are simulated for practice and are not experimental measurements.
